Septic tank rules in England

Under the Environment Agency's General Binding Rules, a septic tank must not discharge directly into a river, stream, ditch or other surface water. Since 1 January 2020, tanks that did so had to be replaced or upgraded, for example to a sewage treatment plant or by connecting to a drainage field. If you are buying or selling a property with a septic tank, the system should be checked against these rules.

If you are not sure whether your system complies, we can inspect it and explain your options.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a septic tank and a sewage treatment plant?

A septic tank separates solids from liquid and lets the liquid soak into the ground through a drainage field. A sewage treatment plant uses a powered process to treat the wastewater to a higher standard, so it can be discharged to a drainage field or, with the right permissions, to a watercourse.

Do you work on off-mains properties?

Yes. We work on rural and off-mains properties with septic tanks, treatment plants and pumped systems around Waterlooville, Denmead and the wider area.
