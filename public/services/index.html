The technology we use

Modern diagnostic and repair technology, including CCTV inspections, no-dig solutions and high-pressure jetting, for accurate results with minimal disruption.

CCTV diagnostics and reporting

High-resolution CCTV gives a thorough inspection and a precise condition report, so problems are diagnosed accurately and fixed cost-effectively from the first visit.

No-dig repair technology

CiPP lining and patch repairs restore damaged pipes from the inside, cutting disruption and cost without traditional excavation.

High-pressure jetting

Water jetting clears even the toughest blockages, restores flow and extends the life of your drainage system.
