Sewer Line Services

Call 0330 633 0336Get a free estimate

Managing sewer line issues can feel complex, but with Elite Drainage Services it does not have to be. Our sewer line services include detailed inspections using advanced video technology and thorough cleaning to remove stubborn blockages.

Persistent odours or ageing sewer lines are addressed through targeted treatments or full replacements where required. Whether responding to an emergency repair or planning preventative upgrades, our solutions prioritise safety, comfort and long-term reliability.

Common sewer line problems

  • Sewage backing up into toilets, baths or the garden
  • Strong sewage smells inside or outside the property
  • Gurgling from several fittings at once
  • Wet, sunken patches of ground above the sewer line

These usually mean the main pipe taking waste from the property is blocked, broken or partly collapsed. Sewage backing up is a health risk and should be dealt with straight away.

Who is responsible for the sewer?

In England, the pipe that carries waste from your property alone and sits within your boundary is normally your responsibility. Since 2011, pipes shared with neighbours or that run beyond your boundary are normally the responsibility of the local water company (Southern Water in most of this area). After a CCTV inspection we can tell you which section is affected, so you know who should pay for the work.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if sewage is coming back up?

Stop using water in the property (taps, toilets, washing machine), keep children and pets away from the affected area, and call us on 0330 633 0336.

Can a sewer pipe be repaired without digging?

Often, yes. Many sewer defects can be repaired from inside using relining or patch repairs. Collapsed sections usually need excavating and replacing.
