Pump Station Works

Call 0330 633 0336Get a free estimate

Pump station works involve the inspection, maintenance and repair of systems that move wastewater or surface water from lower to higher levels to keep drainage networks operating correctly. These systems are essential where gravity drainage alone is not sufficient, and problems can lead to blockages, slow drainage or an increased risk of flooding.

Regular servicing and timely repairs help ensure consistent performance and protect surrounding pipework and property infrastructure. If your system is showing signs of reduced performance or needs scheduled maintenance, get in touch.

What a pump station service covers

  • Check of pumps, float switches and level controls
  • Test of high-level alarms
  • Inspection of non-return valves and rising main connections
  • Removal of rag, fat and debris from the chamber
  • Jetting of the chamber and incoming pipework where needed
  • Report of condition and any parts that need replacing

Signs of a pump station problem

  • The high-level alarm sounding
  • The pump running constantly or cycling on and off
  • Slow drainage or waste backing up
  • Unusual noise or smells from the chamber

Wet wipes and sanitary products are a common cause of pump failure, because they wrap around the impeller. Only toilet paper should be flushed on a pumped system.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a pump station be serviced?

Most manufacturers recommend at least one service a year for domestic pump stations, and more often for commercial or high-use systems. Check your manufacturer's guidance.

My pump station alarm is going off. What should I do?

Reduce water use straight away to stop the chamber overflowing, check the pump has power, and call us on 0330 633 0336.
