Imagine coming home after a long day only to be greeted by a flooded kitchen. It is a nightmare scenario, but one Elite Drainage Services is equipped to handle swiftly. From minor grease build-up to entrenched debris, we use proven methods and modern equipment to restore proper flow efficiently.

Our team is available 24/7 to respond to emergencies, so your home or business returns to normal with minimal disruption.

Signs your drain is blocked

  • Sinks, showers or baths draining slowly or not at all
  • Toilets filling up higher than normal when flushed, or not clearing
  • Gurgling noises from plugholes or the toilet
  • Bad smells from drains, gullies or manhole covers
  • Water pooling around an outside gully or a manhole cover lifting

If more than one fitting in the house is affected, the blockage is usually further down the main drain rather than in a single pipe. That is a job for drain rods or a jetter, not a plunger.

How we clear a blocked drain

  1. Find the blockage. We lift the inspection chambers to see where the water stops flowing, so we work on the right section first time.
  2. Clear it. Depending on the cause we use drain rods or high-pressure water jetting, which cuts through grease, scale, wet wipes and silt and flushes the pipe clean.
  3. Check it. We run water through to confirm everything flows. If a drain keeps blocking, we recommend a CCTV drain survey to find out why, such as a cracked pipe, root ingress or a sunken section.
  4. Leave it clean. We tidy the work area and explain what caused the blockage and how to stop it happening again.

What causes most blockages

Most blocked drains are caused by fat, oil and grease poured down the kitchen sink, wet wipes and sanitary products flushed down the toilet, and tree roots getting into older clay pipes through cracked joints. Leaves and silt also build up in outside gullies, especially after autumn and heavy rain.

A blockage inside your property boundary that only serves your home is usually your responsibility. If the blocked pipe is shared with neighbours or is beyond your boundary, it is usually the responsibility of the water company (Southern Water in most of this area). We can tell you which applies when we inspect it.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer an emergency blocked drain service?

Yes. We provide round-the-clock cover for emergency drainage problems, day or night. Call 0330 633 0336 and tell us what is happening.

How much does it cost to unblock a drain?

It depends on where the blockage is and what is causing it. We give a free, upfront estimate before starting, so you know the price before any work begins.

Can you stop my drain blocking again?

If a drain blocks repeatedly there is usually an underlying cause, such as a damaged or sunken pipe or root ingress. A CCTV survey shows the cause so it can be repaired rather than just cleared.

Yes. We clear blockages in toilets, sinks, showers, baths, outside gullies, inspection chambers and main drain runs for homes and businesses.
