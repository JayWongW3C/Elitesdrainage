CCTV Drain Inspection and Maintenance

Call 0330 633 0336Get a free estimate

Drainage issues do not need to come as a surprise. Our drain inspection and maintenance services help turn drainage performance into something predictable and manageable. With detailed CCTV drain surveys, we provide a clear internal view of your drains without invasive digging.

Preventative maintenance plans help stop blockages before they develop, saving time, stress and unnecessary costs. Comprehensive assessments and written reports keep you informed about the condition of your drainage system, highlighting potential issues and clear next steps.

How a CCTV drain survey works

A waterproof camera on a flexible push rod is fed into the drain through an inspection chamber. Live high-resolution footage is shown on a monitor, and the distance travelled is recorded, so any fault can be located precisely from above ground.

The survey shows problems that cannot be seen any other way, including:

  • Cracks, fractures and holes
  • Root ingress through joints
  • Displaced or open joints
  • Sunken sections (dips that hold water and debris)
  • Build-up of scale, grease or silt
  • Collapsed pipes

When you need a CCTV drain survey

  • Buying a home. See our homebuyer drain survey for £299.
  • Recurring blockages, to find the cause instead of clearing the same drain again and again.
  • Insurance claims, where your insurer needs evidence of damage.
  • Before an extension or building work, to confirm where drains run and their condition.
  • Commercial properties, as part of a planned maintenance schedule to avoid downtime.

What you receive

After the survey you get a written condition report with still images from the footage, the location and depth of any defects, and our recommendations for repair or maintenance. The report is yours to keep and can be shared with your insurer, solicitor, landlord or builder.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a CCTV drain survey take?

Most surveys of a standard house take one to two hours on site, depending on the length of the drain runs and how many inspection chambers there are.

Do I need to be at home for the survey?

We need access to the inspection chambers (manholes) and usually to the inside of the property to run water through the fittings, so someone should be available to let us in.

Can you do planned drain maintenance for my business?

Yes. We offer preventative maintenance, including scheduled CCTV inspections and cleaning, to help keep commercial drainage running without unexpected shutdowns.
