Are you moving home? Do not take the risk of investing in your biggest life purchase without inspecting under the ground. Who knows what surprises you will find once you move in. For just £299 you could save yourself thousands of pounds and hours of distress.

We use top-of-the-range full HD inspection cameras and many years of knowledge to inspect and survey the whole drainage system. Call us for total peace of mind when buying your new home.

Why a standard house survey is not enough

A RICS HomeBuyer Report or building survey looks at the parts of a property that can be seen. Surveyors generally lift manhole covers at most; they do not put a camera inside the drains. Damaged drains can be expensive to repair, and once you have completed the purchase the cost is yours.

A drain survey before exchange of contracts gives you the facts while you can still act on them.

What the survey includes

  • Full HD CCTV inspection of the accessible drain runs
  • Check of inspection chambers, gullies and connections
  • Location of any defects such as cracks, roots, displaced joints or collapses
  • Written report with images and our recommendations
  • An estimate for any repairs needed

How the report helps you

If the survey finds a problem you can ask the seller to repair it before completion, negotiate a reduction in the price to cover the work, or decide not to proceed. If the drains are in good condition, you can buy with confidence.
